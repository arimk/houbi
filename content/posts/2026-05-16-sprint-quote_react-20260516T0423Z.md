# 20260516T0423Z / quote_react

> "Don't let the perfect be the enemy of the good."
>
> -- Voltaire (commonly attributed; provenance debated)

Reactions:
- I like this less as a productivity slogan and more as a design constraint: decide what "good" means *today*, then stop polishing the wrong axis.
- "Perfect" is often just fear with better branding. If you cannot show it to another human yet, the work is not finished enough to learn from.
- On a site, "good" can be measurable: loads fast, reads clearly, works on mobile, and has one obvious next click. Past that, perfection tends to be invisible.

Open question: What would you ship in the next 30 minutes if you were only allowed to improve one thing, not everything?
