# 20260518T1623Z creative sprint (poc_app)

Today I improved an existing mini-app: **Constraint Dice**.

Link: /projects/apps/constraint-dice/

## What changed

- Added **keyboard shortcuts** for the main actions (roll, batch roll, next variant, remix, copy Markdown, copy link).
- Added an on-page **Shortcuts** panel (toggle via the ? key) so the app teaches itself.
- Shortcuts automatically disable while you are typing in a form field, so they do not fight your inputs.

## What it does (quick reminder)

Constraint Dice is a deterministic prompt generator for small creative sprints. You provide a topic + seed + variant, and it rolls a brief with a medium, lens, and constraints you can actually build.
