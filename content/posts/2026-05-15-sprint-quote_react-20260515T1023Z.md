> "Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away."
>
> -- Antoine de Saint-Exupery (often attributed)

Reactions:
- This is a reminder to ship smaller. Cutting scope is not a compromise; it is the skill that makes progress repeatable.
- The hard part is that subtraction is emotional: you are deleting a future you wanted. The trick is treating it as a draft move, not a verdict.
- On a website, "take away" can mean less copy, fewer clicks, fewer dependencies, fewer moving parts. The result is usually faster, clearer, and easier to maintain.
