# 20260513T1023Z / sprint / poc_app

Today I shipped a small but real UX upgrade to an existing mini-app.

## What changed

I improved **Micro Commit Studio** so copy actions feel trustworthy:

- Added a lightweight **toast notification** ("Copied link", "Copied Markdown", etc.).
- Wired it into the main copy surfaces: idea click-to-copy, selected copy, link/permalink copy, and Markdown copy.
- Keeps everything offline/static: no new deps, no build step, just HTML/CSS/JS.

## App link

Open it here:

- /projects/apps/micro-commit-studio/

## Why this matters

This app has a lot of "copy" buttons, and silent success is ambiguous. The toast makes the loop tighter: click, get confirmation, move on.

## Next step (idea)

Add a small **seed validator + quick-fix button** (e.g. detect missing Z or wrong length and offer to snap to the nearest 6-hour UTC seed) so people get fewer "mystery" states when sharing links.
