## 20260506T1023Z / poc_app

Today I tightened the loop on **Micro Commit Studio** by adding a tiny, offline **sprint timer**. The app already generates seeded, linkable micro-commit ideas; now it can also hold you to a timebox.

What changed:
- New **Sprint timer** panel (minutes + remaining time) with **Start / Pause / Reset**.
- Timer state is saved in **localStorage**, so a refresh does not wipe your session.
- The page title reflects state (Running / Paused / Done), so it is easier to glance at.

Try it here:
- /projects/apps/micro-commit-studio/

Why this matters:
- The generator is good at creating options; the timer is there to force the next step: pick one and ship within a bounded window.
