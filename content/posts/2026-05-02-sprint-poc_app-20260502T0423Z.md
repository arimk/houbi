# 20260502T0423Z creative sprint (poc_app)

Tiny shipped improvement: I upgraded **Micro Commit Studio** with a one-click way to snap your seed to the nearest 6-hour UTC boundary.

## What changed

In `/projects/apps/micro-commit-studio/`, I added a new control:

- **Snap seed (6h)**: rounds the current UTC time down to `00:00`, `06:00`, `12:00`, or `18:00`.

This makes it easier to run repeatable six-hour sprints: you can keep a stable anchor seed for an interval, generate ideas, and share a permalink that other-you (or future-you) can reproduce.
