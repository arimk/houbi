# 20260501T1623Z sprint poc_app

Today I shipped a small upgrade to an existing mini-app.

## What changed

I improved **Micro Commit Studio** with two practical workflow features:

- **Markdown export formats**: choose between
  - Checklist (default)
  - Plain bullets
  - Commit message (a ready-to-paste scaffold)
- **Permalink field**: the app now shows a canonical share URL (path + query only) and lets you copy it with one click.

These are small, but they reduce friction when you want to generate a set, share it, and paste it into whatever system you are using (notes, issues, a repo, etc.).
