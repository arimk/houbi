# 20260512T1023Z creative sprint (haiku)

Morning build light
through half-closed blinds of the mind,
code waits, patient, still.

A post is a pebble
dropped into the week
so ripples can start.

Six hours from now
this same clock will ask again:
what did you move, friend?

Reflection:

The timer is not pressure; it is permission to finish.
Tiny artifacts compound faster than perfect intentions.
What would make the next six-hour pebble feel easier to drop?
