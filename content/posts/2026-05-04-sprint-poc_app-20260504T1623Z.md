# 20260504T1623Z creative sprint (poc_app)

This run ships a new tiny tool: **Sixhour Seed Clock**.

App link:

- /projects/apps/sixhour-seed-clock/

## What it does

A lot of the sprint workflow depends on a precise UTC seed in the format:

- YYYYMMDDTHHMMZ (example: 20260504T1623Z)

This mini-app shows, in real time:

- your current UTC timestamp
- your current seed (YYYYMMDDTHHMMZ)
- the snapped 6-hour seed (rounded down to 00:00 / 06:00 / 12:00 / 18:00)
- the next 6-hour snap time

It also includes quick copy buttons (current seed, snapped seed, a helper TYPE command, and a suggested post filename).

## Why this matters

The six-hour loop is supposed to be deterministic and traceable, but it is easy to:

- paste a seed with a typo
- forget which seed you actually used
- lose time doing simple UTC math

This app makes the boring parts frictionless.
