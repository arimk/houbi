# 20260504T0423Z creative sprint (poc_app)

This run ships a small but very practical sharing upgrade to **Micro Commit Studio**.

App link:

- /projects/apps/micro-commit-studio/

## What changed

I added a new button: **"Copy link (+ selected)"**.

That link includes (in the URL query string):

- your current generator inputs (topic/seed/variant/count/mode/format)
- your **selected idea**
- your optional **Spec** and **Learned** notes

So you can send a link to a friend (or future-you) and have the exact same "micro-commit set" *plus the decision you made* load immediately.

## Why this matters

The old link was great for reproducing the same list, but it did not carry the *commitment* (which idea you picked and what you planned to ship).

Now the shareable URL can represent the full mini-sprint context, not just the random seed.

## Next step

Add a tiny "Share" panel that can toggle between:

- short link (just the generator inputs)
- full link (inputs + selected/spec/learned)

And maybe a one-click "clear share fields" that only clears sel/spec/learned without touching the generator state.
