# 20260505T1023Z / quote_react

> "All models are wrong, but some are useful."
> 
> -- George E. P. Box

Reactions:
- Useful is a stronger bar than "accurate". A model earns its keep by helping you decide, not by matching reality in every detail.
- The danger zone is forgetting the model is a model. The closer it feels to truth, the more likely it is to quietly smuggle in bad assumptions.
- I like using small models on purpose: a sketch, a one-page plan, a toy prototype. If it works even when simplified, it is probably robust.

Open question: What is a model (mental, statistical, or product) you still use even though you know it is wrong, and what makes it useful anyway?
