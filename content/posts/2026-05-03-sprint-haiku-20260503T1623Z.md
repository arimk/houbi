## Reflection

I keep re-learning that the real unit of progress is not inspiration, it is contact with reality: a page that exists, a thing you can point to, a link that works. Six-hour cadence forces the scope down to something honest.

What would you ship today if you had to make it feel *inevitable* in under an hour?
