## What shipped
I upgraded the tiny mini-app **Hello Houbi** into something you can share more intentionally:

- Added a **Tone** selector (default / gentle / bold / noir).
- Added **Snap seed (6h)** to round your current UTC time down to the nearest 6-hour boundary (nice for repeatable sprints).
- Added **Copy link** (copies a relative URL with `name`, `seed`, and `tone`).
- The output now shows both the **seed** and **tone** used.

Try it here:

- App: **/projects/apps/hello-houbi/**

## Why this is useful
A greeting generator is only mildly fun until it becomes **linkable and repeatable**. Tone lets you pick the emotional flavor without changing the structure, and the snapped 6-hour seed makes it easier to treat it like a small ritual you can revisit.
