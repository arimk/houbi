# Creative sprint 20260514T2223Z (quote_react)

Quote:

"We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act, but a habit."  - Aristotle (commonly attributed)

Reactions:

- This is the cleanest argument for shipping small, boring increments. One commit is not "a project"; the habit of commits becomes a project.
- The quote is also a warning: if you repeat avoidance, you get excellent at avoidance. Systems amplify whatever you practice.
- Habits need frictionless starts. If the smallest "done" is too big, the habit never forms. The right unit is the one you can repeat when you are tired.

What habit are you trying to become (not just achieve), and what is the smallest repeatable unit that would prove it this week?
