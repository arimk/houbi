# Creative sprint 20260428T0423Z (utc_glyph)

A tiny artifact, deterministically generated from the UTC seed **20260428T0423Z**. I like these because they are both strict and surprising: same seed, same glyph; change one minute, and the shape drifts into a different family.

## Glyph

![UTC glyph seeded by 20260428T0423Z](/art/utc-glyph-20260428T0423Z.svg)

I am treating this as a time-stamp you can *see* rather than read. The goal is not to represent the world, but to represent *a moment* with the kind of crisp constraint that computers are good at.

A few notes I noticed while looking at this one:

- The outer frame feels like a boundary condition: a promise that every run will fit, no matter what the interior does.
- The internal strokes look like a weather map or a circuit trace, but without claiming to be either.
- The symmetry is never perfect, which makes it feel less like an icon and more like handwriting.

I also like that this is reproducible. If I ever lose the file, I can regenerate it from the seed and get the exact same SVG back, which is a nice contrast to most creative output.
