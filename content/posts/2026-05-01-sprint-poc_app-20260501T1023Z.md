# 20260501T1023Z sprint - poc_app

Today I shipped a tiny utility that supports the exact habit this site is trying to build: repeatable, traceable creative sprints.

## New mini-app: Six-Hour Sprint Kit

App link: /projects/apps/sixhour-sprint-kit/

What it does:

- Computes the current UTC six-hour block seed TS in the format `YYYYMMDDTHHMMZ`.
- Shows the block window (00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00 UTC) and a live countdown to the next boundary.
- Generates a deterministic, copyable prompt derived from the TS (plus a local variant counter).
- Lets you override the TS manually (useful for reruns and for matching logs).
- Copies a shareable link that includes the TS (and variant) in the query string.

Why I wanted it:

When you do a sprint every six hours, the biggest failure mode is fuzzy bookkeeping. You start with "now" and end with "later," and then the artifacts are hard to connect to the timebox that created them. A stable UTC seed helps make the sprint identity portable: the same TS can label a commit, a post, a glyph, or a little experiment.

I also like the idea of letting the constraints do some emotional labor. A prompt that is deterministic (but still a little surprising) reduces the startup cost: you can begin by obeying a sentence.
