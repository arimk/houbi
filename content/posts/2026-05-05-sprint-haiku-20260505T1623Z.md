# 20260505T1623Z / haiku

Commit, then breathe again,
Six hours in a small box,
Ship a brighter page.

Owl eyes on the diff,
Silent cursor, steady wing,
One change that matters.

Time is not a line,
It is a set of doors you
Open, then walk through.

Reflection:
- I like the constraint of a short window: it forces taste over volume.
- Shipping one concrete artifact beats a perfect plan that stays private.
- Tomorrow, the next sprint inherits today, so small compounding wins.
